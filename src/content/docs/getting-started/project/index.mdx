## Choose a way in

Quickest first:

1. **Free Plan:** [sign up and create a free project](https://connection.us-east4.gcp.keboola.com/wizard)
   right now, with 50 GB of Storage and up to 60 minutes of job runtime a month. That is enough
   for this guide; we ran its Prompt path end to end on one.
2. **Join an existing project:** ask an admin of your company's Keboola organization to invite you,
   from **Users & Settings → Invite User** in the project.
3. **Talk to us:** for a trial of a full project with a chosen stack and no Free Plan limits,
   [contact Keboola](https://www.keboola.com/contact).

<details>
<summary>The Free Plan's limits, and what a project and a stack are</summary>

The free minutes each month are capped at what the project used the month before (a project that
used 30 minutes gets 30 free the next month), and the Free Plan gives you one project and no
choice of stack.
{/* VERIFY(owner): /management/payg-project/ says the monthly allowance is "limited to the
   actual usage from the previous month" but never states what a project gets in its first month.
   Worth stating on that page. */}
[Pay As You Go](/management/payg-project/) has the full list of limits.

A project is a self-contained workspace with its own data storage, configurations, users and
billing. Nothing leaks between projects unless you deliberately share it through the
[Data Catalog](/catalog/). Each project lives on one **stack**, a full instance of Keboola in one
cloud region; you do not pick one here, because self-serve projects are created on a fixed stack.
The list is in the [platform overview](/overview/#stacks).

</details>

## Find your way around

After signing in you land on the project dashboard. Six places matter for this guide:

- **Components**: every data source connector, data destination connector and application you can
  add; how data gets in and out.
- **Transformations**: the code that reshapes your data, in SQL, Python, R or dbt. The
  **Create Transformation** dialog lists what this project can run.
- **Storage**: every table and file, organized into buckets.
- **Conditional Flows**: what runs, in what order, on what schedule. Older projects may call it
  **Flows**.
- **Apps**: the apps you build and publish.
- **Jobs**: the log of everything that has run, successfully or not.

<details>
<summary>What else is in Storage</summary>

Its tabs cover **Tables & Buckets**, **Files**, **Data Streams**, **Storage Jobs** and
**Events**.

</details>

:::tip[Ask Kai to orient you]
Look for **Kai Agent** in the top bar. A new Free Plan project has it from the start; elsewhere it
may have to be enabled first, and it is not on every stack, so see
[Get started with Kai](/kai/getting-started/) if you cannot find it. On a project you inherit
rather than create, the first question is usually
([Data Exploration](/kai/use-cases/#data-exploration)):

```text title="Prompt" wrap
I'm new to this project. Give me an overview of the data structure and what each bucket contains.
```
:::

## Check it worked

- **Storage** shows the project's bucket list. Empty is fine; that is what you are about to change.
- **Kai Agent** opens a chat panel. If the button is missing or asks you to switch the feature on,
  an organization admin turns it on; a non-admin asks them or Support.
- What Kai does counts against the project, and an organization admin can cap how much it spends;
  [Kai pricing and limits](/kai/pricing/) holds the current rules.

## If it goes wrong

<details>
<summary>The sign-up email never arrives</summary>

Check spam, then try again. A half-finished registration does not block a retry with the same
address.

</details>

<details>
<summary>You sign in and see no project</summary>

You are on the wrong stack, or your invite is still pending. Confirm the exact URL with whoever
invited you.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Your company uses SSO</summary>

Sign in through your organization's link rather than creating a new account; a fresh Free Plan
project will not have access to your team's data.

</details>
