If you are a third-party developer working with Keboola, chances are that you do not have access to a
project in Keboola. Having a project is not strictly necessary, but it helps: you can test your code in action.

You can apply for one if you do not belong to a company that already has a Keboola project, you will
use it fairly rather than for production, and you stay active in the development.

Once you [register](/extend/component/tutorial/#before-you-start) (and join a vendor) in the
[Developer Portal](https://components.keboola.com/), you will gain access to a development project. If you
don't have one, or need a development project for other reasons, [send us an email](mailto:support@keboola.com).
Not into creative writing? Feel free to use our template:

> Hello,
> I'm *XY* (from the *YZ* company) and I'd like to develop an *extractor | writer | application* for Keboola.
> My component will do *some really awesome things*.
